This RNA-seq experiment examines the transcriptional response of blood-stage
Plasmodium falciparum parasites to artemisinin exposure. Samples S1 and S2
were cultured in the presence of 10 nanomolar artemisinin for six hours
before harvest. Samples S3 and S4 were cultured in parallel under identical
conditions but without any drug, serving as untreated controls. All four
cultures were harvested at the same six-hour timepoint.
